ABOUT THE FOUNDATION

Progress begins when women help shape the way forward.

Mahila Shakti Vikas Foundation is built around a simple idea: opportunities work better when women can choose what is useful to them.

Explore our focus areas
WHO WE ARE

Start by listening. Make the next step practical.

Women know their own lives best. Their time, responsibilities, interests and ambitions should help shape the support offered—not be treated as obstacles to someone else's plan.

Our community-focused work brings people together around useful learning, hands-on skills, wellbeing awareness and livelihood choices. The aim is to make information easier to use and leave room for each woman to decide what comes next.

OUR VISION

A future where women and girls can learn, take part, make informed choices and pursue goals on their own terms.

WHAT GUIDES US

Respect is part of the work, not an extra.

These commitments influence how activities are planned, how people are welcomed and how progress is understood.

01

Listen with respect

Make room for lived experience, different perspectives and the priorities women name for themselves.

02

Keep choices open

Share clear information and practical options without deciding in advance which path is right for someone.

03

Learn together

Value local knowledge and invite community participation when shaping useful activities.

04

Be accountable

Communicate honestly, protect people's dignity and represent outcomes only when they are verified.

PRACTICAL AREAS OF FOCUS

Different interests call for different starting points.

These are areas the foundation can explore with communities; each activity should reflect local needs and available support.

LEARNING

Knowledge for everyday decisions

Learning support, useful information and digital confidence can help open up choices.

SKILLS

Practice with a purpose

Hands-on skills can connect personal interests with possible work and livelihood options.

COMMUNITY

Support shaped together

Shared ideas and peer connections can help people find relevant next steps.

A CONVERSATION CAN BE A BEGINNING

Bring a question. Share a skill. Find a way to take part.

Every community is different. We welcome conversations about what women and girls would find useful where they live.
